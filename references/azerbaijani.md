# Azerbaijani motivation letters

Read this file before writing or editing a letter in Azerbaijani.

## Register
- Use the formal "siz" form throughout. Never use "sən".
- Keep the tone polite and direct. Azerbaijani business writing is more formal than English, so avoid slang.
- Use standard Azerbaijani with correct letters: ə, ı, ö, ü, ç, ş, ğ. Never replace them with ASCII lookalikes.

## Greetings and closings
- Greeting with a name: "Hörmətli [Ad Soyad],"
- Greeting without a name: "Hörmətli işə qəbul komandası,"
- Closing line: "Müraciətimə baxdığınız üçün təşəkkür edirəm."
- Sign-off: "Hörmətlə," then the full name.

## Useful opening patterns
- "[Şirkət] şirkətində [vəzifə] vakansiyasına müraciət edirəm."
- "[Şirkət] şirkətinin [vəzifə] vakansiyasını gördüm və təcrübəmin bu rola uyğun olduğunu düşünürəm."

## Style traps
- Do not translate English sentences word by word. Rewrite each idea in natural Azerbaijani word order (subject, object, verb at the end).
- Keep sentences short. Azerbaijani sentences grow long fast with participle chains, so split them.
- Avoid heavy Russian or English borrowings when a common Azerbaijani word exists. Keep established technical terms (for example "kibertəhlükəsizlik", "şəbəkə", "giriş nəzarəti") and keep product or standard names in their original form.
- Use "təcrübə" (experience), "məsuliyyət" (responsibility), "nəticə" (result) in plain, concrete sentences.
- The same punctuation rules apply: no em dash and no semicolons.

## Bilingual requests
If the user asks for both languages, write the English version first, then the Azerbaijani version as an independent rewrite, not a literal translation. Check that facts and numbers match in both.
